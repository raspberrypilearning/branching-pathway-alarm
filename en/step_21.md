## 2A - Design your own case

What should your alarm look like? Sketch a shape, try materials or lay out your parts.

> [!CAUTION]
>
> Have you completed [1A - Connect and test your Pico](1A%20-%20Connect%20and%20test%20your%20Pico.md)? Do that before testing any circuit. You can sketch now and return.

## Step 1

> [!TASK]
>
> Choose a size and shape. Where will the sensor, warning and Pico fit? You can change your mind as you build.

## Step 2

> [!TASK]
>
> Try a quick shape or position. Leave room for the Pico, USB cable, input wires and warning light or buzzer. Keep the circuit removable while you test.

## Test

> [!TASK]
>
> Try the shape with your parts, or paper shapes the same size. Can the sensor reach what it detects and can someone notice the warning?

Choose any parts from **Detect a change** and **Give a warning**. Their pages have the wiring and code.
