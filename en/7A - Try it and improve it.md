## 7A - Try it and improve it

Test whatever you have so far: an idea, a case, one component or a working alarm.

> [!CAUTION]
>
> Have you [tested your Pico](1A%20-%20Connect%20and%20test%20your%20Pico.md)? Stop the program and unplug before moving wires.

## Choose a test

- **An idea:** act it out. What should start and stop the warning?
- **A case:** try the shape. Can the sensor and warning do their jobs?
- **A component:** try its code. Does it respond the way you expect?
- **An alarm:** try the trigger and reset. Does it also stay quiet when it should?

## Change one thing

Try a different position, material, setting or rule. The component pages have wiring diagrams and code if you need help.

## Test

Repeat your test. Keep the change if it helps, or try another idea. You can come back here whenever you like.
